This site and the kiosk

In-Space Power keeps two public faces. They are not the same face, and the company prefers it that way.

inspacepower.com — this site

The company statement: the cycle's figures, generation mix, tariffs, payment ledger, and the curated notice stream above. Edited, metered, and issued by the company itself.

kiosk.inspacepower.com — the notice board

The public record as posted: complaints, counters, corrections, and the open docket, in the order they were posted. Nothing on the kiosk is polished. Everything on it counts.

The rule is old: in public, the company is what it posts. House business stays indoors. A notice that was not posted did not happen in public — no matter how true it is.

House channels

Not everything the company says is said in public. House channels carry the internal traffic — shift handoffs, settlement arguments, draft statements that never post. None of it appears on the kiosk. What follows is what has leaked.

Figures are rounded. The company does not confirm the existence of the channels whose traffic is summarized here.
